If you know me, you probably know that my brain is always on. There’s always a thought running and its never a dull moment in my head. I could be staring at a wall and have a 100 situations run in my head at that point. I never really thought about this until i was 20 when i started realizing that how i think varied from how my friends (we all are unique but you get it 0 vs 100 thoughts). if your brain’s always on, you’re benefitting from this.

sometimes its good and sometimes its really not. Its helpful when you’re working in engineering and you’re trying to figure out all the possibilities behind why your code broke. its not helpful when you’re working in engineering and you’re trying to figure out why you’re building something extremely complex for handling a niche scenario that never happens ever. or that you’re prepping for an interview and you’re nervous and you’re trying to think of every question that could be thrown at you.

suppressing my thoughts stopped working here because i would always be back at square one - overthinking again later. but then a few years of trial and error later, i learned to accept that its cool. i have a steady stream of thoughts (infinite water yaay) and i can direct it however i want it, and it builds some beautiful, beautiful things.

so one thing this also does for me is makes me extra anxious. now i’m not just thinking about existing scenarios, i’m coming up with new doomsday scenarios by the second, and there’s also a fear of “what if there’s an unknown unknown waiting to do bad for me”.

this doesn’t come up on the normal days, but absolutely does for an interview for example. the scope of questions that can come at you is infinite, and you cannot prepare for infinite in under a week.

what i realized at one point was, this is all energy. we run on calories, thinking takes effort and effort takes energy. i can spend this energy thinking about bad scenarios or i can spend it thinking about good scenarios. shifting to the other direction in my head, felt pretty simple, imagine you’re a tree growing deep into the soil, and now instead, someone flipped you around and now you’re growing towards the sun and everything bright and warm instead.

<TreeFlip />

as soon as this shift happened, my brain first felt weird thinking about accepting positive outcomes because of how conditioned it had gotten to planning for the negative outcomes. once it started accepting the positive outcomes, the thoughts just started flowing in a positive direction. from

just like how we think there’s bad unknowns waiting to screw us over, there’s a lot more good unknowns to help us through because we’ve also really come into this life through a series of good knowns (read up on the odds of genetic mutations its really crazy), so there’s more good things that we do not know of, helping us succeed more than bad things that we know and don’t know of pulling us down.

so yes, i appreciate you, my mind for keeping me sane and running for all this years so far. i appreciate you, my mind for keeping me insane and running from now till god knows when.
